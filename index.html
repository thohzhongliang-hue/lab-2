Introduction

The Malaysian Red Crescent Society, legally established organization under various Acts of Parliament, including the Federation of Malaya Incorporation) Act 1962, the Malaysian Red Cross Society (Incorporation) Act 1965, the Change of Name Act 1975, and revised Act 540 in 1995. Its structure is based on the 1949 Geneva Conventions, to which Malaysia is committed.

Our Core Service

Training Institute

Equipping individuals and organizations with life-saving skills and the knowledge to provide mental and emotional support during crises.

Ambulance Services

Rapid and reliable emergency medical response available nationwide.

Disaster Response

Mobilizing resources and trained personnel to provide immediate relief and recovery assistance during natural and man-made disasters.
